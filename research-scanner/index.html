Grant Farm Research Scanner

Operated by The Grant Farm

The Grant Farm Research Scanner is a developing research tool designed to help The Grant Farm conduct preliminary research on publicly available information published by nonprofit organizations. It is not yet in use on outside websites.

It is intended to support our paid diagnostics, used only after a client's deposit is in, and test scans we authorize to check accuracy. It is not for screening prospects.

What does the scanner do?

During its initial research stage, the scanner is designed to:

  • Check a website's robots.txt instructions before accessing pages and respect applicable crawling restrictions.
  • Read the text of a public page selected by The Grant Farm.
  • Do so without logging into accounts, submitting forms, or attempting to access restricted areas.

The scanner will not attempt to bypass crawling restrictions. If the website's instructions prohibit access by the scanner, or its crawling policy cannot be determined reliably, the affected pages will be marked Not assessed, with the reason documented.

How it identifies itself

GrantFarmResearchScanner/0.1 (+https://grant-farm-landing.vercel.app/research-scanner)

Data handling

In its initial testing stage, the scanner is designed to display extracted page text locally for review. During this initial testing stage, it will not save collected website content, submit it to an AI service, or use it to train AI models.

These practices may change as the tool develops. This page will be updated before its research or data-handling practices change.

Website owners

Website owners who have questions, concerns, corrections, or opt-out requests regarding the scanner may contact The Grant Farm using the business contact information below.

To request that the scanner not access your website's pages, you may add the following instruction to your robots.txt file:

User-agent: GrantFarmResearchScanner
Disallow: /

The scanner is designed to follow this instruction. A robots.txt file is not a technical access control, so it does not block crawlers that ignore it.
