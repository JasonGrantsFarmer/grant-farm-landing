About

Funding is grown,
not found.

Most nonprofits I work with don’t have a development department. They have a director who’s also writing grant applications at 11pm.

I join as the part of your team that keeps funding coming in and keeps donors coming back. You work with me directly, from the first list of possible funders to the final report, so nothing gets lost in a handoff and every proposal still sounds like you.

  • 01

    Grants

    Finding funders who fit your mission, writing proposals that make the case clearly, and sending reports on time so each renewal is easier than the last.

  • 02

    Donor retention

    Thank-yous, updates and a stewardship plan that turn first-time givers into people who give again next year, and the year after.

  • 03

    Working together

    A clear scope, plain language and a calendar you can see. I work around your team’s schedule, not the other way round.

Planting season is year-round.
